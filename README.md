# My_first_hackathon
## Me and my boys may fail today, but never in the future.
        My first hackathon (SaaSathon) was held at SSN Engineering College. I was really excited because it was my first hackathon, and it held for 36 hours. The excitement I had at the beginning was not the same at the end—but that experience taught me a lot.
        This hackathon showed me how important preparation is and what kind of pre-work we need to do before participating.
        This repository contains everything I worked on during SaaSathon. I now have a clear idea of what to improve and how to approach future hackathons better.
